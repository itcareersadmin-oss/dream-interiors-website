INTERIOR MATERIAL

uPVC Interiors

At Dream Interiors & Traders, we use uPVC as our primary material for interior works. It is used for applications such as modular kitchens, wardrobes, storage units and other customized interior solutions.

Why Choose uPVC?

uPVC offers a practical combination of durability, low maintenance and long-term value for interior applications.

Waterproof

Resistant to water and moisture.

Termite Proof

Resistant to termite-related damage and deterioration.

Budget Friendly

A practical and cost-effective solution for interior applications.

Recyclable

uPVC can be recycled and reused through appropriate recycling processes.

Eco-Friendly Choice

Its durability, long service life and recyclability can help reduce frequent material replacement and waste.

Fire-Retardant

uPVC has fire-retardant properties compared with many conventional combustible interior materials.

uPVC vs Conventional Interior Materials

OUR PROCESS

From Your Idea to Final Handover

01

Free Site Visit

Our team visits your site to understand the space, requirements and project needs and takes the necessary measurements.

02

Planning & Design

Based on your requirements, we prepare the required 2D plans and 3D designs and make necessary revisions based on feedback and approval.

03

Quotation

After understanding and finalizing the project requirements, we provide a detailed quotation covering the scope of work, rates and relevant project details.

04

Construction

After approval, construction work begins according to the finalized drawings, specifications and project scope.

05

Interiors

Required interior works including modular kitchens, wardrobes, false ceilings, wall panels, TV units, furniture and other interior works are executed according to the finalized requirements.

06

Final Inspection

The completed work is inspected to verify the agreed scope, finishing and project requirements before handover.

07

Handover

After final inspection, the completed project is handed over to the client along with applicable warranty and support details.
