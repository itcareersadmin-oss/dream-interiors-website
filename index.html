Frequently Asked Questions

Do you undertake both construction and interior projects?

Yes. We handle complete planning, civil construction and full interiors under one roof. You can choose the full turnkey project or only the specific phase you need.

Do you provide a free site visit?

Yes. Book a visit and our team will come directly to your site anywhere across Telangana to take measurements and understand your requirements.

Do you provide services across Telangana?

Yes. We accept projects across the entire state of Telangana, with branch offices located in Karimnagar (Head Office), Gangadhara, Sircilla and Hyderabad.

Can I choose only interior services?

Absolutely. You can choose only interiors (modular kitchens, wardrobes, false ceilings), only construction, or the complete turnkey package.

Which material do you use for interiors?

We primarily specialize in high-grade uPVC because it is 100% waterproof, termite-proof, fire-retardant, long-lasting and significantly lower maintenance than wood.

How can I get a quotation?

Simply book a free site visit or send an enquiry. After understanding your space and design choices, we share an itemized quotation with clear scope and specifications.
